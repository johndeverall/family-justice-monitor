# Incorporation Checklist for Family Justice Monitor NZ

## Pre-Incorporation Steps

### 1. Review and Finalize Constitution
- [ ] Review the draft constitution with all founding members
- [ ] Seek legal review to ensure compliance with Incorporated Societies Act 2022
- [ ] Make any necessary amendments
- [ ] Ensure alignment with planned charitable registration requirements

### 2. Recruit Founding Members
- [ ] Recruit at least 10 founding members (required minimum)
- [ ] Ideal: Recruit 15-20 founding members to ensure quorum and engagement
- [ ] Ensure diversity of perspectives aligned with governance goals:
  - [ ] Legal expertise in family law
  - [ ] Research and methodological expertise
  - [ ] Child welfare expertise
  - [ ] Lived experience representatives
  - [ ] Māori representation
  - [ ] Pacific peoples representation
  - [ ] Financial/operational expertise
- [ ] Obtain written consent from each founding member
- [ ] Collect contact details for member register

### 3. Hold Founding Meeting
- [ ] Schedule founding meeting with all founding members
- [ ] Prepare agenda:
  - [ ] Adopt the Constitution
  - [ ] Elect initial Committee members (7-9 people)
  - [ ] Elect officers (Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer)
  - [ ] Appoint Contact Person (usually Secretary)
  - [ ] Approve registered office address
  - [ ] Authorise application for incorporation
- [ ] Take accurate minutes of meeting
- [ ] Have all founding members sign the Constitution

### 4. Prepare Application Documents
- [ ] Complete incorporation application form
- [ ] Prepare signed Constitution with all founding member signatures
- [ ] Identify registered office address (NZ physical address required)
- [ ] Identify Contact Person (must be ordinarily resident in NZ)
- [ ] Prepare list of initial Committee members with contact details

## Incorporation Application

### 5. Submit Application to Registrar
- [ ] Submit online via https://www.societies.govt.nz or
- [ ] Submit by post to Registrar of Incorporated Societies
- [ ] Pay incorporation fee (check current fee on Registrar's website)
- [ ] Ensure all documents are correctly completed

### 6. Address Any Registrar Queries
- [ ] Respond promptly to any requests for clarification or amendments
- [ ] Make any required changes to Constitution
- [ ] Resubmit if necessary

### 7. Receive Incorporation Certificate
- [ ] Receive Certificate of Incorporation from Registrar
- [ ] Note the incorporation date and society number
- [ ] File certificate securely

## Post-Incorporation Steps

### 8. Establish Banking and Financial Systems
- [ ] Open bank account in society name (will need incorporation certificate)
- [ ] Set up accounting system
- [ ] Establish financial controls and authorisation procedures
- [ ] Obtain receipts book/system if accepting donations

### 9. Register for Tax Purposes
- [ ] Register with Inland Revenue for IRD number
- [ ] Determine tax obligations (likely exempt if charitable)
- [ ] Set up GST registration if required (likely not initially)

### 10. Apply for Charitable Registration (Planned)
- [ ] Review constitution to ensure it meets Charities Act 2005 requirements
- [ ] Complete charitable registration application via https://www.charities.govt.nz
- [ ] Provide required documentation:
  - [ ] Copy of Constitution
  - [ ] Certificate of Incorporation
  - [ ] Details of Committee members
  - [ ] Financial forecast/budget
  - [ ] Description of planned charitable activities
- [ ] Wait for assessment (can take 3-6 months)
- [ ] Address any queries from Charities Services

### 11. Develop Operational Policies
Develop and adopt policies for:
- [ ] Health and Safety
- [ ] Privacy and data protection (Privacy Act 2020 compliance)
- [ ] Conflict of Interest
- [ ] Financial Management
- [ ] Research Ethics and Methodology
- [ ] Anonymisation and confidentiality procedures (Family Courts Act 1980 s11B compliance)
- [ ] Volunteer management
- [ ] Complaints handling
- [ ] Document retention and management

### 12. Set Up Administrative Systems
- [ ] Establish Member Register system
- [ ] Create filing system for meeting minutes and decisions
- [ ] Set up email and communication systems
- [ ] Create website/update existing website with Constitution and governance info
- [ ] Set up secure storage for confidential documents

### 13. Insurance
Consider appropriate insurance cover:
- [ ] Public liability insurance
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance (particularly for research activities)
- [ ] Directors and Officers (Committee members) insurance
- [ ] Contents/assets insurance if applicable

### 14. Ongoing Compliance Requirements
Set up systems to ensure:
- [ ] Annual General Meeting held within 6 months of financial year-end
- [ ] Annual financial statements prepared
- [ ] Annual Return filed with Registrar (within prescribed timeframe)
- [ ] Annual Return filed with Charities Services (once registered)
- [ ] Member Register kept up to date
- [ ] Constitution amendments filed within 20 working days
- [ ] Notification of any Committee changes to Registrar
- [ ] Notification of any Contact Person changes to Registrar

## Initial Strategic Planning

### 15. Establish Initial Governance Framework
- [ ] Schedule regular Committee meetings (minimum quarterly)
- [ ] Develop annual work plan
- [ ] Create strategic plan (3-5 year vision)
- [ ] Establish key performance indicators
- [ ] Define roles and responsibilities clearly

### 16. Funding Strategy
- [ ] Develop funding model
- [ ] Identify potential funders (trusts, foundations, individual donors)
- [ ] Prepare funding applications
- [ ] Create donor acknowledgment systems
- [ ] Establish transparency protocols for funding disclosure

### 17. Research Framework
- [ ] Develop research ethics framework
- [ ] Establish research methodology standards
- [ ] Create protocols for data collection and storage
- [ ] Develop anonymisation procedures (Family Courts Act compliance)
- [ ] Establish peer review processes
- [ ] Plan initial research projects

### 18. Stakeholder Engagement
- [ ] Identify key stakeholders
- [ ] Develop engagement strategy
- [ ] Consider Advisory Council establishment
- [ ] Build relationships with complementary organisations
- [ ] Engage with Māori and Pacific communities

### 19. Communications and Transparency
- [ ] Update website with:
  - [ ] Constitution
  - [ ] Committee member bios
  - [ ] Annual reports (when available)
  - [ ] Financial statements (when available)
  - [ ] Funding sources
  - [ ] Research methodology
- [ ] Develop media/communications policy
- [ ] Create regular reporting mechanisms to members

## Timeline Estimate

| Phase | Estimated Duration |
|-------|-------------------|
| Constitution review and founding member recruitment | 2-4 weeks |
| Founding meeting and application preparation | 1-2 weeks |
| Registrar processing | 4-8 weeks |
| Post-incorporation setup | 2-4 weeks |
| Charitable registration application | Submit within 3 months |
| Charitable registration approval | 3-6 months from application |

**Total to incorporation:** Approximately 2-3 months
**Total to charitable registration:** Approximately 6-12 months

## Notes
- The Incorporated Societies Act 2022 came into effect on 5 October 2023
- All existing incorporated societies must re-register by 5 April 2026
- Ensure all founding members understand their commitment and responsibilities
- Consider having a lawyer review the Constitution before filing
- Keep detailed records of all meetings and decisions
- Maintain transparency with members throughout the process

## Questions to Resolve

Before proceeding, ensure clarity on:
1. Who will serve as interim officers (Chair, Secretary, Treasurer)?
2. What will the registered office address be?
3. Who will be the Contact Person?
4. What is the initial funding source for incorporation fees and initial operations?
5. What are the immediate priorities post-incorporation?
6. When do you want to hold the first proper Committee meeting?
7. What is the timeline for launching first research activities?
